# Manage sections

A page is a stack of **sections**: a hero, a list of APIs, an FAQ and so on. This page covers adding, reordering, renaming, hiding and deleting them. To change what's *inside* a section, see [Edit a section](04-edit-section-content.md).

![The section list](images/section-list.png)

1. **Drag handle**: drag a row up or down to reorder.
2. **More menu (⋯)**: rename or delete a section. It appears when you hover a row.
3. **Show/hide**: the navigation bar and footer can be hidden, but not moved or deleted.
4. **Add (+)**: adds a section to the end of the page.

## Add a section

**Between two sections, from the canvas**
1. Hover the canvas between two sections. A blue **Add section** button appears on the line between them.
2. Click it. The section library opens in the panel.
3. Click a section. It's added at that spot and opened for editing.

![Add section appears between sections on the canvas](images/canvas-inserter.png)

**At the end of the page**
1. Click **Add section** at the bottom of the panel, or **+** next to the page name.
2. Click a section in the library. It's added at the end of the page.

**By dragging**
1. Open the library (either way above).
2. Drag a card onto the canvas and drop it between two sections.

![The section library](images/section-library.png)

1. **Hint**: reminds you that you can click or drag.
2. **Library**: every section you can add to this page. Hover a card to see a one-line description.

## Reorder sections

Do either of these:
- In the section list, drag a row by its handle (⋮⋮) and drop it above or below another row.
- Select the section on the canvas and use the **↑** and **↓** arrows in its tag (see below).

The navigation bar and footer always stay at the top and bottom.

## Select a section

Click a row in the section list, or click the section on the canvas. The section is outlined, a tag appears above it, and its settings open in the panel.

![A selected section and its tag](images/section-selected.png)

1. **Section tag**: the section's name and layout, then **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**.
2. **Breadcrumb**: shows where you are. Click **←** or the page name to go back to the section list.

## Duplicate a section

Select the section and click **Duplicate** (⧉) in its tag. The copy appears directly below the original, with the same content and settings.

## Rename a section

Section names only appear in the builder. Visitors never see them. Renaming helps when a page has two sections of the same type.

1. Hover the row and click **⋯ → Rename**.
2. Type the new name and press **Enter** (or **Esc** to cancel).

## Hide the navigation bar or footer

Click the eye icon on the **Navigation Bar** or **Footer** row. Hidden chrome keeps its content, so click the eye again to bring it back. This applies to every page that has a navigation bar and footer.

## Delete a section

Do any of these:
- Hover the row and click **⋯ → Delete**.
- Select the section and click **Delete** (🗑) in its tag.
- Open the section and click **Delete section** at the bottom of its settings.
- Select the section and press **Delete** or **Backspace** (when you're not typing).

A message confirms the deletion. Click **Undo** within a few seconds to bring the section back with everything in it.

![Deleted, with Undo](images/delete-undo.png)

> **Tip:** you can also undo any change with **⌘Z** / **Ctrl+Z**, even after the message has gone.
