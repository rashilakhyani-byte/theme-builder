# Portal Builder guide

The Portal Builder is where you design your developer portal: its home page, its pages, its look and its navigation. You pick a starting template, arrange ready-made sections, edit their content in place, set your brand, preview on any device and publish.

![The builder: top bar, left panel and canvas](images/overview.png)

1. **Top bar**: undo and redo, switch between **Edit** and **Preview**, and **Publish Changes**.
2. **Panel switch**: choose the page you're editing, or open **Themes & Typography**.
3. **Section list**: every section on the page, in order. Click one to edit it.
4. **Canvas**: your portal exactly as visitors will see it. Click anything on it to edit.
5. **Add section**: opens the section library.
